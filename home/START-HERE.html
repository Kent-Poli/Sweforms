HOME MAINTENANCE SYSTEM

START HERE

Your simple start to a home that lasts.

APP ADDRESS: https://sweforms.se/home/

1. Open your app

Open the app address supplied by the seller in Safari on iPhone/iPad or Chrome on Android. Use a regular browser tab, not private browsing.

2. Add it to your home screen

iPhone/iPad: tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then confirm Add. If shown, enable Open as Web App. Android: open the Chrome menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On a computer, use your browser’s install button if available. Menu names may vary.

3. Build your maintenance plan

Choose starter tasks that suit your home, or select New maintenance task. Enter a name, category, last completed date, next due date or repeat interval, cost and note. Suggested intervals are examples: follow the manufacturer’s instructions and adapt them to your home. Use one currency consistently; amounts have no currency symbol.

4. Mark completed work

Select Done. Enter the completed date, actual cost and an optional note. Recurring tasks receive a new due date calculated from the completed date. For a task without an interval, set a new date or leave it finished. Every completed record is saved in History.

5. Save a backup

Open Guide & backup and select Download backup. Keep the file somewhere safe. Restore backup replaces your entire current plan and history. Back up regularly and before changing devices or the app address.

Good to know

No account or cloud sync. Your data is saved on your device, in the browser you use. Clearing website data or private browsing may cause data loss. On some devices, the home screen app uses separate storage: import your backup there. After the first visit, the app works offline. It does not send reminders while closed.
